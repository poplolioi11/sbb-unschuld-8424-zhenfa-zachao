# 診法雜抄（柏林國家圖書館 Slg. Unschuld 8424） / Zhenfa zachao [Miscellaneous Notes on Diagnostic Methods]: Transcription Dataset

《診法雜抄》是柏林國家圖書館 Slg. Unschuld 8424 所藏漢文手抄摺帖的館方擬題。正文從脈象分類、對欄分表與〈脈歌〉到傷寒辨論，保存條列、欄旁小字及篇題。可用於研究脈診術語與醫學知識在便攜抄冊中的編排。

This Literary Chinese pocket-size manuscript, catalogued as Slg. Unschuld 8424 at Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin, organizes observations of the pulse and discussion of historical medical categories in tables, lists and mnemonic verses.

館方79幅全件盤點，正文第2至35幅共34幅逐區轉錄，另定位空葉、書套、色卡與淡痕。共627項版面單元。

All 79 scans are inventoried. The image-based transcription covers scans 2–35, with 627 layout units; blank areas, casing and capture reference are recorded.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

逐幅回圖，比對獨立讀稿及原初錄，採用 59 項回圖訂正。疑讀、藏印或細小批注按實際可辨範圍保留。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。按原圖由右至左分欄，分表按左右區與子欄。保留側註、字形異體與字讀候選。館方 ALTO 僅供同底本查重及比較，公開文字取自原圖。

Source-image review compared independent observations with the initial transcription and records 59 image-supported changes. Uncertain readings remain marked. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. Reading order follows the physical right-to-left text and table subdivisions. Uncertain characters and small annotations are marked. Existing library ALTO is noted for comparison.
